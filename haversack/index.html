Haversack Challenge — Pack Your 60-lb Load

Interactive Puzzle

Pick 10 Items That Total Exactly 60 lb

Soldiers carried clothing, shelter, tools, food, water, ammunition—and more—on the march. Choose exactly ten items from the list. Your goal is a pack that weighs exactly 60 pounds. Some weights are rounded to whole pounds for gameplay; a few small items are marked “approx.”

Item descriptions follow National Park Service lesson materials on Civil War soldier accouterments. Major gear weights are adapted from historical load analyses; small-item weights are approximations for gameplay. See Sources & Notes below.

Sources & Notes
  • NPS: “Accouterments of a Civil War Soldier” (Gettysburg) — item types such as knapsack, haversack, tinware, gum blanket, shelter half, and sewing kit.
  • Civil War Monitor: “The Weight They Carried” — typical weights for major gear and clothing.
  • NPS (Appomattox): Common Soldier Activity — rifle-musket gear and ammunition carried.
  • Springfield Model 1861 — firearm weight (grouped with accouterments in this game).
  • S & S Sutler: Shelter Tent Halves — shelter-half equipment; game weights are approximated.
  • Foods of the American Civil War — ration background for hardtack, coffee, and sugar.

Items marked “approx.” use rounded values to keep gameplay simple while remaining historically plausible.
